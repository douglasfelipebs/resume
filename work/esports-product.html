Case study · Gamers Club

Backend leadership on a high-traffic platform

Software Engineer · July 2021 – June 2023 · Sorocaba, SP (Remote)

Context

Gamers Club runs a high-traffic SaaS platform for competitive gaming. I led the design and development of backend services there, working closely with Product and Engineering.

Problem

The platform's backend had to keep up with heavy traffic while the product kept changing. The team was also growing, so engineering quality and delivery practices had to scale along with the code.

Constraints

Services ran as distributed systems on Kubernetes, behind REST APIs other teams depended on. Product goals were measured in conversion metrics, so technical work had to connect to those numbers, not only to the codebase.

What I owned

  • Leading the design and development of scalable backend services.
  • Designing and maintaining REST APIs and distributed systems.
  • Mentoring a team of six engineers through code review, knowledge-sharing sessions, and day-to-day guidance.
  • Defining technical initiatives with Product and Engineering leadership.
  • Technical interviews, onboarding new engineers, and technical presentations on backend architecture, engineering practices, and software quality.

Decisions

  1. Design for the long run. REST APIs and distributed services were built for scalability, maintainability, and long-term reliability, because other teams and heavy traffic depended on them.
  2. Tie technical initiatives to product metrics. Planning with Product and Engineering leadership kept engineering work pointed at conversion, without trading away quality or delivery consistency.
  3. Invest in people and operations together. Mentoring, interviews, and onboarding raised the team's bar, while better observability with Grafana, Sentry, and CloudWatch, plus deployment and CI/CD improvements, raised the platform's.

Result

  • Technical initiatives that contributed to a 20% increase in key product conversion metrics.
  • Six engineers mentored, plus new engineers interviewed and onboarded.
  • Better operational reliability through observability, monitoring, deployment processes, and CI/CD pipelines.

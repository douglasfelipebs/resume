Case study · Framework Digital · VOLL

Event-driven services for corporate travel and expenses

Software Engineer · September 2023 – July 2024 · Belo Horizonte, MG (Remote)

Context

VOLL is a SaaS platform for corporate travel management and expense automation. I worked there as a contractor through Framework Digital, on the backend services.

Problem

Companies booked business flights and vehicles through separate airline and rental providers. The platform needed those bookings in one place, and business expenses needed a simpler path than reimbursing scattered purchases.

Constraints

Bookings came from external airline and rental partners, so the platform depended on integrations it did not control. Services talked to each other across a distributed system, and some work had to finish asynchronously instead of inside a request.

What I owned

  • Design and development of scalable backend services for the platform.
  • Design and implementation of the integrations that centralised corporate travel bookings.
  • Building and maintaining GraphQL APIs and Kafka-based event-driven services.
  • Contributing to the architecture and planning of a centralised virtual corporate card.
  • Taking part in architecture discussions and team engineering standards.

Decisions

  1. Centralise bookings behind the platform. Integrating airline and rental providers directly brought flights and vehicles into one booking flow.
  2. Make asynchronous boundaries explicit. GraphQL, through Apollo Server, served the request API, and Kafka carried work between services, so slow or partner-dependent steps did not block the caller.
  3. Hold the quality bar with TDD. Test-Driven Development, automated tests in Jest, code review, and continuous integration on Bitbucket Pipelines kept the services reliable as they changed.

Result

  • About 1,000 to 2,000 business flight and vehicle reservations per month going through the integrations.
  • Reliable communication and asynchronous processing across the platform's distributed services.
  • Architecture and planning input on a virtual corporate card designed to support hundreds of thousands of Brazilian reais in transactions.
